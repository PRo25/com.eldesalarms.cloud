Adds support to Eldes Security Cloud to manage Eldes Alarm Systems

ELDES refresh tokens and alarm PINs are encrypted with an encryption passphrase you choose during pairing. The passphrase is not stored by the app. After a Homey app restart, repair the device and enter your ELDES account credentials, alarm PIN, and passphrase again. If you forget the passphrase, repair the device and sign in to ELDES again with a new one.

The arming status supports Disarmed, Armed, and Home Armed. Arm, Stay Arm, and Disarm are separate buttons shown together. The device also displays ELDES's active-fault and unaccepted-partition-alarm states. Existing alarm devices are migrated to the generic device class, and the legacy Homey alarm-state capability is removed to prevent the selector UI. ELDES-reported status history uses two device Timeline capabilities: Armed/Disarmed and Stay Armed/Stay Disarmed. Armed updates only the first capability to true; Stay Armed updates only the second to true; Disarmed updates both to false. Successful Arm, Stay Arm, and Disarm requests sent by Homey are recorded as separate, command-specific entries in that device Timeline. Status is checked every 30 seconds by default. Change the polling interval in the device's Advanced Settings (5-300 seconds).

ELDES API request and response diagnostics are enabled by default and can be disabled in the device's Advanced Settings. They include sanitized request/response data; passwords, PINs, passphrases, authorization values, and tokens are redacted.
